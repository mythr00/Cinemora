The Hongqi Bridge in Sichuan Province opened to traffic in April 2025.
The structure spanned the Zumuzu River near Maerkang in the Aba prefecture.
In November 2025, officials closed the bridge after cracks appeared on the approach road.
A landslide on the mountain slope then caused part of the western approach to collapse.
No deaths were reported because the bridge had already been evacuated.
Investigators examined whether reservoir water at Shuangjiangkou had weakened the fractured rock.
Geological surveys and the nearby hydropower project became part of the public debate.
National Highway 317 traffic was diverted while engineers assessed the damage.
