title: Genres and voices
link: /create
keywords: genre, category, voice, narration, catalogue, packs

A project's category selects its genre pack: the research query
templates, source hints, pacing, graphics motifs and entity
handling the production uses for that kind of documentary (for
example true crime, engineering, history or general documentary).
The genre catalogue is served from the installed packs, so what
you can choose on the New production page is exactly what the
pipeline has.

Narration voices come from the installed voice catalogue — the
voices actually present on the server. The default voice is used
unless a production's options name another installed voice.
Narration runs at the voice model's native rate; Cinemora does
not adjust narration speed.
