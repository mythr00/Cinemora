The Graphics tab shows the motion
graphics a production generated: the designed elements that carry
facts on screen — stat cards, name and date cards, timelines,
diagrams, lower thirds and evidence presentations.

Graphics are built from the script's verified facts (names,
dates, numbers) from a library of designed templates. Two rules the
design system enforces: a graphic is a designed element, never a
full narration sentence printed on screen; and provenance (file
names, source labels, watermarks) is never burned into a graphic —
it lives in the certificate and the rights manifest instead.

Where a sentence has no verified footage, a graphic may carry the
beat, and the asset report records how much of the film is
graphics versus direct or related footage.
