The Assistant answers from your real Cinemora records —
projects, productions, asset decisions, reports — and can search
the web and public video and media sources for resources,
returning real links.

Things the assistant does fall into three groups:

- Questions it answers immediately: your account, projects,
  productions, failures, outputs and the product itself.
- Changes it prepares for your confirmation: creating a project,
  submitting or retrying a production, approving a plan,
  recording a plan decision, requesting a revision render,
  saving a preference. The assistant prepares the exact
  operation and shows it to you; nothing runs until you press
  Confirm.
- Consequential changes (explicit two-step confirmation):
  cancelling a production. The confirmation card is marked as
  consequential and needs a deliberate two-step confirm.

Pending actions expire after 15 minutes. Rejecting or letting an
action expire means nothing was executed. Every tool call the
assistant makes — reads included — is recorded in your assistant
activity, and each answer shows the tools it used.

The assistant only ever sees your own data. It has no access to
external accounts: no Google Drive, YouTube or cloud-storage
connections exist in Cinemora yet, and the assistant will say so
rather than pretend to check them.
