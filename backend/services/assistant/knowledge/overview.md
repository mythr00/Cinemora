Cinemora is a documentary production studio: you provide a script,
and the production pipeline researches, plans, retrieves and
verifies real footage and images, designs motion graphics,
narrates, and renders a finished documentary.

The main areas, from the left rail:

- Dashboard: your projects as cards, with each project's
  production stage progress and any plan-review prompts waiting
  for you.
- Projects: the full list of your documentary
  projects.
- New production: start a documentary from a script.
- Assistant: this assistant — ask about your projects
  and productions, search for resources, and prepare operations
  for your confirmation.

A project page has tabs: Overview, Plan, Assets,
Graphics, Edit and Certificate. Everything about one documentary —
its script, plan, asset decisions, outputs and provenance — lives
on those tabs.
