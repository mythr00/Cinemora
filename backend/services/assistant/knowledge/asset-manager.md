The Asset Manager lets you review the visual materials selected
for your project: every visual the production considered for the
latest run, what was decided, and why.

For each candidate asset you can see:

- The decision: accepted (used in the film), rejected, or missing
  (no verified visual could be found for that sentence).
- The verification verdict: the checks the asset passed — topical
  match, frame-level verification and OCR where applicable — and,
  for rejections, the recorded reason (for example a contradictory
  place or subject, or a named person who is not the subject).
- Rights: the licence evidence recorded for the asset. Where
  licence metadata is available for an asset, it is recorded
  verbatim; where it is not, the licence honestly stays
  "unknown" with a verify-before-publish status.

Discovery searches the open web, public media archives and
video sources for footage and images. Candidates whose metadata
merely fails to mention the required subject are not
automatically rejected — they can be admitted as possible
matches, but only become usable when frame verification
confirms them.

The Asset Manager is read-only evidence: to change a visual, use
the Plan tab's replace decision, or re-run the production.
